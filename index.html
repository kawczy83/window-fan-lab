How the model works

Airflow depends on open windows, fan placement, wind pressure, and the indoor/outdoor temperature difference. A fan needs a second opening for a through-path. The particles show airflow across the open windows; the status describes the main streams. Relative airflow is measured against the model’s reference maximum, not in air changes per hour.
